# 🔔 Doorbell

**1 Ghost. 4 Defenders. One pot.**

Doorbell is a **5-player asymmetric wagering game built on Solana**. At the start of every match, one player is randomly chosen as the **Ghost**, and their identity is revealed to everyone.

The other four players must defend their own dorms by spending in-game coins to build **guns, mines, and gates**. The Ghost's objective is to break through these defenses and eliminate the defenders, while the defenders work together to protect their dorms and survive.

### 💰 On-Chain Wagering

Players wager **SOL** to enter the game. The wagers are held in a **Solana vault** throughout the match and are distributed according to the final game outcome.

### 🎮 Core Gameplay

* 👻 **1 Ghost vs 4 Defenders**
* 🔫 Build guns and defenses
* 💣 Place mines and traps
* 🚪 Build gates to protect your dorm
* 🪙 Manage in-game resources
* 💰 Wager SOL and compete for the pot
* ⚡ Game outcomes and rewards handled on Solana
